# Phase 5: Brief says when it cuts - Plan

## Goal

With a long project path and a small brief budget, the brief's fixed lines use up the budget and its quoted turns are dropped whole with no mark, and three core tests fail when `TMPDIR` is a long path (#46). The brief must say when it cut, and the tests must not depend on the temp path's length.

## Must be true when done

- At a budget that leaves a quoted turn no room, that turn's line in the brief reads its label followed by `...` (`It last asked: ...`), one mark per turn, and that turn is not among the briefed turns in the session's state file.
- For a session whose quoted turns are each under 240 characters, so the uncut brief holds no `...`, every budget from 0 to the uncut brief's length gives no brief, the uncut brief byte for byte, or a brief that contains `...`. A budget whose room after the frame cannot hold more than the mark gives no brief and writes nothing.
- A new test with a long project path fails against `brief.rs` as it was at `de82912` and passes after the change.
- An uncut brief is unchanged: no file under `crates/terminus-core/tests/goldens` changes, and the golden and byte-identity tests pass.
- `cargo test --workspace --all-features` passes with the default `TMPDIR` and with `TMPDIR` set to an existing 130-character directory, and `git diff de82912 -- 'crates/*/tests'` removes or changes no line inside an `assert!`, `assert_eq!` or `assert_ne!` call.
- Neither `docs/design/0007-injection.md` nor the `brief.rs` doc comments still say a quotation with no share is dropped, and `CHANGELOG.md` has a Fixed entry for #46 under `[Unreleased]`.
- Issue #46 is closed by the merged change (human-verify: needs the merged PR on GitHub).

## Context

- Locked: D-01 both silent paths (a share of 0 and a share of 1 to 3) are the defect; D-02 a dropped turn keeps its label and shows `recall::excerpt::ELISION` alone; D-03 the marks render even when they push the body over, and the backstop in `frame` cuts the pointer's tail; D-04 a room that cannot hold the mark renders nothing; D-05 `clip` does not change; D-06 a mark-only turn is not recorded as briefed; D-07 the head line and pointer keep their text; D-13 no golden moves; D-14 the docs that become false change in this phase.
- Tests: D-09 allows edits to test setup and fixture lines and never to assertions or goldens; D-10 says how each test stops depending on the path; D-11 adds the five terminus-crate tests; D-12 sets the regression test's shape.
- Out of scope: `clip` and the rest of `inject/mod.rs`, the prompt arm's output, the excerpt window in `recall/excerpt.rs`, the head line's and pointer's text, and any change to an uncut brief or a golden file.
