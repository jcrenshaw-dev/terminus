## Scope boundary

In: The brief's two silent cut paths, a quoted turn dropped whole at a share of 0 and a share of 1 to 3 characters returned raw by `clip` with no mark, both give way to a marked cut in `inject/brief.rs` (#46). The brief renders nothing when the room after the frame cannot hold the mark. The three core tests #46 names and the five terminus-crate tests with the same cause stop depending on the temp path's length, by editing setup and fixture lines and no assertion. ## Decisions

- D-01 (Both silent paths): Both silent cuts are the defect. A quoted turn whose share is 0 is dropped whole, and a share of 1 to 3 characters goes through `clip`'s `budget <= marker` branch, which returns raw characters with no `...`. Measured 2026-10-20 with long `TMPDIR`s: at 85 characters the brief keeps the head line and the pointer, drops both quotes and holds no `...`. The failure is now at `inject_brief.rs:753`, not the issue's `:728`, because that assertion arrived in `2f1fc4b` after #46 was filed. Evidence: `crates/terminus-core/src/inject/brief.rs` (render `:238-259`, `within` `:346-360`, `shares` `:386-393`), `crates/terminus-core/src/inject/mod.rs:167-184`, `crates/terminus-core/tests/inject_brief.rs:725-779`.
- D-02 (The mark): A turn dropped for lack of budget keeps its label and renders as `recall::excerpt::ELISION` alone (`It last asked: ...`), one mark per turn. One mark for the whole exchange was rejected because it does not say which turn was cut, and the existing assertion at `inject_brief.rs:753-756` expects the label to survive a cut. Evidence: `crates/terminus-core/src/recall/excerpt.rs:42-47`, `crates/terminus-core/src/inject/mod.rs:167-172`, `crates/terminus-core/tests/inject_brief.rs:753-756`, `:775-778`.
- D-03 (Mark before pointer tail): The marks are a fixed cost measured with the head line and the pointer. When head line, pointer and marks exceed the room, the marks are still rendered and the existing backstop in `frame` cuts the pointer's tail, adding its own `...`, so no cut brief is silent. Dropping the pointer's tool-names line first was rejected. Evidence: `crates/terminus-core/src/inject/brief.rs` (`bare` `:329-344`, render `:243-259`), `crates/terminus-core/src/inject/mod.rs:446-460`.
- D-04 (Lower edge): When the room after the frame cannot hold `...`, the brief renders nothing, as it already does when the frame itself does not fit. Keeping 1 to 3 raw characters was rejected because that brief says nothing and hides the cut. This moves hardin phase 1 D-06's line by the mark's length, and the 0007 sentence about "no room for a single character" changes with it. Evidence: `crates/terminus-core/src/inject/mod.rs:437-460`, `.planning/_archive-hardin/1/CONTEXT.md:23`, `docs/design/0007-injection.md` (budget paragraph), `crates/terminus-core/tests/inject_brief.rs:831-872`.
- D-05 (`clip` unchanged): `clip` stays as it is and the fix lives in `brief.rs`. `clip` is shared with the prompt arm and the backstop, and the decision record counts each turn's characters from its output. Evidence: `crates/terminus-core/src/inject/prompt.rs:286-309`, `crates/terminus-core/src/inject/mod.rs:454-460`.
- D-06 (Not briefed): A turn shown only as the mark is not recorded in `Brief::turns`. Only turns whose text reached the brief are, so the prompt arm does not refuse an unseen turn as `carried_by_brief`. Evidence: `crates/terminus-core/src/inject/brief.rs` (`remember` `:144-164`, `quoted` `:267-282`, `:252`), `crates/terminus/tests/suppression.rs:589`.
- D-07 (Fixed lines unchanged): The head line and the pointer keep their current text, the project path named twice included, at every budget. Evidence: `crates/terminus-core/tests/inject_brief.rs:915-921`, `crates/terminus-core/tests/goldens/recall-projections.txt` (both `== brief` blocks), `docs/design/0007-injection.md` (brief template), `crates/terminus-core/src/inject/brief.rs:238-242`.
- D-08 (Fixtures, not a product bug): The `redacted_recall` and `inject_prompt` failures come from the fixtures. The prompt arm quotes a 240-character window that starts a third of the window before the first query token, and the `Edit` projection puts the absolute path, which sits under the temp dir, first. Redaction runs over the full projection before the window is cut, so a long path leaks nothing. Measured 2026-10-20: `redacted_recall` fails at `:378` and `:422` at 85, 110 and 130 characters; `inject_prompt` fails at 130 only, at `:732`. Evidence: `crates/terminus-core/src/recall/excerpt.rs:34-40`, `:187-195`, `:265-287`, `crates/terminus-core/tests/redacted_recall.rs:283-313`, `crates/terminus-core/tests/inject_prompt.rs:39-45`, `:732-735`.
- D-09 (Test edits allowed): This phase drops the cycle's added-lines-only rule for test setup and fixture lines, and keeps it for assertions and golden files. Adding lines alone cannot meet roadmap criterion 2: `inject_prompt.rs` builds its temp dir with `tempfile::tempdir()` directly, and at a 130-character `TMPDIR` the brief test's head line and pointer alone come to 568 characters against a room of 500. Narrowing criterion 2 instead was rejected. Hardin phase 1 D-06 is the precedent. Evidence: `crates/terminus-core/tests/inject_prompt.rs:39`, `crates/terminus-core/tests/redacted_recall.rs:50`, `crates/terminus-core/tests/inject_brief.rs:29`, `:739`, `crates/terminus-core/src/testkit.rs:325-338`, `.planning/_archive-hardin/1/CONTEXT.md:23`.
- D-10 (How each test stops depending on the path): The brief test sizes its budget from the measured fixed lines of the uncut brief, as it already measures `frame_cost`; its root is never created. The prompt-arm tests put their asserted text (the planted credential, `lanternFlicker`) where the 240-character window reaches it at any path length. A short temp root outside any repository was rejected because it ignores `TMPDIR`, which a sandbox may set because `/tmp` is not writable, and a root inside the checkout resolves to the repository through git. Evidence: `crates/terminus-core/tests/inject_brief.rs:8-11`, `:713-719`, `crates/terminus-core/src/testkit.rs:119-133`, `crates/terminus-core/tests/redacted_recall.rs:284-285`.
- D-11 (Five more tests): The five terminus-crate tests that fail at a 130-character `TMPDIR` for the same cause get the same fix here: `decisions.rs:265`, `prompt.rs:213` and `suppression.rs:477`, `:605` and `:781`. Each writes `session-edits.jsonl` under `tempfile::tempdir()` and is cut by the same window. Measured 2026-10-20: 62 test binaries passed and 6 failed under `cargo test --workspace --all-features --no-fail-fast`. Filing them separately was rejected, because the phase goal says the tests must not depend on the temp path's length and the Claude Code sandbox's `TMPDIR` is already 79 characters. Evidence: `crates/terminus/tests/prompt.rs:50`, `:213`, `crates/terminus/tests/suppression.rs:65`, `:477`, `:605`, `:781`, `crates/terminus/tests/decisions.rs:55`, `:265`.
- D-12 (Regression test): The regression test is new lines in `inject_brief.rs`. It uses a long project name that is never created on disk, as `no_brief_exceeds_the_ceiling_whatever_the_config_says` does, and sizes its budget from the measured fixed lines. It asserts the mark on the dropped turn's own line, because a quote longer than 240 characters already ends in `...`. Evidence: `crates/terminus-core/tests/inject_brief.rs:781-821`, `crates/terminus-core/src/recall/excerpt.rs:279-285`.
- D-13 (No golden moves): At the default 6,000-character budget the brief takes the uncut early return, so the mark appears only on a cut and no golden file or byte-identity test changes. A golden change is the sign the mark leaked into uncut briefs. Evidence: `crates/terminus-core/src/inject/brief.rs:229-236`, `crates/terminus-core/src/config.rs:228`, `crates/terminus-core/tests/goldens/recall-projections.txt`, `crates/terminus-core/tests/recall_golden.rs:40`.
- D-14 (Docs in-phase): The same change updates what it makes false: the `render`, `within`, `bare` and `quoted` doc comments in `brief.rs`, the budget paragraph in `docs/design/0007-injection.md` ("a quotation whose share is nothing is dropped"), and a Fixed entry under `[Unreleased]` in `CHANGELOG.md`, as phase 2 did. Each changed doc is opened in RustRover for John to review. Evidence: `docs/design/0007-injection.md`, `CHANGELOG.md:10`, `.planning/phases/2/SUMMARY.md:17`, `:35`, `crates/terminus-core/src/inject/brief.rs:189-210`, `:346-352`.
